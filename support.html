Before you send

Describe what you were doing and what happened. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and Warrabee version from Settings → About & Privacy Policy. Hide personal details in screenshots. App records and receipts are not attached automatically.

What's free?

Save products, receipts, and warranties; search your collection; open original receipt files; and set warranty reminders. No Warrabee account is required.

What does Pro add?

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase for Claim Pack and manual Backup & Restore. The price is shown by the App Store before purchase. It is not a subscription. Automatic backup and iCloud sync are not included.

Restore a purchase or a backup?

Restore Purchases checks Pro access for your Apple Account. To import a backup of products and receipt files, use Backup & Restore. Existing products are not replaced by restore.

Missing records or a receipt?

Contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app. Your records are stored on the device. If you have an exported backup, keep an untouched copy while investigating.

About your coverage

Warrabee organizes records; it does not provide insurance, extend warranties, or guarantee claim acceptance. Coverage is decided by the seller or warranty provider.
